## Open topics

### Kubocd alternative

Adaltas presented an alternative to Kubocd that eliminates the need for custom controllers by relying entirely on Helm for template and dependency resolution through an umbrella chart paired with library charts. The [okdp-lib-charts](https://github.com/OKDP/okdp-lib-charts) and [platform-charts](https://github.com/OKDP/platform-charts) repositories have been created to test this approach.
